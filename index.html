Before we begin

A few useful answers.

How do I request a consultation?

Email a short description of your project, your goals and any deadline. We can discuss the scope, deliverables, availability and fees before work begins.

Do you offer online tutoring?

Online sessions use Microsoft Teams or Zoom. Contact me with your preferred dates, time zone and learning goals. Scheduling, materials and payment arrangements can be discussed directly.

How are scope and fees agreed?

Describe the work, expected output and timeline in your inquiry. Scope, availability, fees and applicable terms are discussed directly before work begins.

Where can I download Darkelf browsers?

Visit darkelfbrowser.com for downloads and release notes. Distribution-specific capabilities and privacy behavior are documented in the relevant project repository.

Does this website use tracking or cookies?

This website’s code includes no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party widgets, JavaScript or cookie storage. Images and styles are loaded locally.

GitHub Pages hosting infrastructure may process connection information. External links and communication services have their own privacy practices.
